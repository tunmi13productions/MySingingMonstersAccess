# Tips

My Singing Monsters shows a tip on every loading screen, and the mod reads each one aloud. They go by quickly and you only hear one at a time, so I've collected all of them here (144 in the game's English text), sorted by topic. Where a tip mentions something you'd normally find by looking at the screen, I've added how to reach it with the mod.

Keys mentioned below are listed in the README. The ones you'll use most: Tab and Shift+Tab move, Enter presses, Escape goes back, O lists everything on your island, M opens Places, and A says your status. Each letter also has a Control+Shift version. On a list of 20 items or fewer, the number keys reach an item directly (Shift+1 to Shift+0 for items 11 to 20): press once to hear it, twice to press it.

## Monsters

- It takes 4 feedings to level up a Monster. With the mod, select the monster and Tab to FEED. It says how far along you are and what it costs, for example "Feed, 2 of 4 towards level 10, 3,840 food". On a monster's bar, FEED is usually number 7.
- Higher level Monsters produce more Currency and improve breeding chances.
- Always place a Monster before selling it to gain XP.
- A Monster's Likes become visible when it is placed on an Island. With the mod, the island list (O) says how many of a monster's known Likes are met, and Info (I with a monster selected) describes it. One Like is always a mystery, and the game keeps it that way.
- There are many different types of Monster: check out the Stats tab in the Info menu to identify a Monster's class. The mod also says the class when an egg hatches, for example "Class: Natural".
- Monsters bought with Diamonds do not require incubation.
- Rare and Epic Monster eggs can be identified by special shimmers when they're incubating. You don't have to see the shimmer: when you move to a Nursery with a Rare or Epic egg in it, the mod says so.
- Double the size of your favorite singing denizens with the Biggify feature! It's BIGGIFY on a selected monster's bar, and the mod says when it's on sale.
- Not all Monsters appear on all Islands. Check the Collection button to view an island's native Monster species population. COLLECTION is in the island's Tab list.
- Don't know how to get a Monster you're missing? Tap it in the Book of Monsters to learn more! The Book of Monsters is in Places (M). breeding-guide.md also lists what to breed, island by island.
- Some Monsters, like Dipsters or Werdos, require special Currencies such as Keys or Relics to collect them.

## Sound and the island's song

- Use headphones for 3D sound! On its own, the game hears everything from the camera high above the island, so nearly every monster sounds centred. The mod spreads them out: a monster on the left of the view sings in your left ear. Control+Shift+S changes how wide the spread is, and selecting a monster (which zooms in on it) spreads its neighbours further.
- Mute Monsters to vary an Island's song. MUTE is on a selected monster's bar, and the island list says "muted" for a muted monster.
- Muted Monsters still earn Currency. So muting one costs you nothing.
- You can adjust the volume of each individual Monster! The game only shows the volume slider while you're moving a monster: select it, press MOVE, then Shift+Comma and Shift+Period lower and raise its volume, and Shift+Home and Shift+End go to the lowest and highest. Then press Enter to put the monster down; the same spot is fine.
- Group duplicate Monsters together for some added volume!
- The upgraded Castle produces a bass sound.
- You can save the tempo of each Island's Time Machine! The Time Warper is a slider too, using the same Shift+Comma and Shift+Period keys.
- Some Decorations in View Mode make sounds when tapped. View Mode is "Hide menus (view mode)" in the island's Tab list.

## Getting around the game

- Got a question about something? Visit a Help menu to get answers. Many screens and buildings have a HELP button. Tab to it and press Enter.
- Check out View Mode in the bottom left corner. With the mod it's "Hide menus (view mode)" in the island's Tab list, wherever it sits on screen.
- New items will appear in Market menus as you level up! The Market is in Places (M).
- Decorations are ordered by price and categorized in the Market!
- The Mailbox is where you can get news about special offers and promotions, and receive gifts from the Monster-Handlers! MAILBOX is in the island's Tab list and in Places (M).
- There are lots of Currencies in the Monster World, but luckily they are color-coded! You don't need the colours: the mod says each currency's name, such as coins, diamonds, food, shards, relics or keys. A says your main ones.
- Don't want to wait? Use Diamonds to speed things up. Be careful with any button that spends diamonds. The mod reads the price before you press it.
- You will gain XP from baking, removing obstacles, and upgrading Structures. X says how much XP you have towards the next level.

## Your island and buildings

- Remove Obstacles to make more room on your Islands. F gives advice on which tree or rock to clear first, and REMOVE on a selected tree or rock says its price.
- You can buy Obstacles as Decorations in the Market!
- Customize the size of your Decorations by using the slider while moving them around your island! The size is a slider: Shift+Comma and Shift+Period.
- Start baking something in your Bakeries, and then use the Rebake feature to repeat the order!
- Enhanced upgrades for your Breeding Structure and Nursery shorten waiting times by 25%!
- Check out the Hotel and Storage structures in the Market at Level 15! The Hotel's OCCUPANTS and the Storage Shed's CONTENTS both say how full they are.
- Upgrades to the Grand Hotel and Storage Warehouse mean even more Monster Occupants and space for Decorations!
- What's better than Paradise? The Crystalline Castles, with distinct colors and looks on each Island!
- Get special Island Skins to redecorate your Islands and enjoy awesome Boosts!
- Premium items like Plant Island's Maximum Mine are unlocked after the first in-app purchase.
- Limited-time Seasonal Decorations can be placed on Islands other than their native Seasonal Event's Island!

## Wishing Torches

- Unlock the Wishing Torches at Level 12!
- When you light a Wishing Torch, it improves the chances of breeding special Monsters!
- The more Wishing Torches you have lit on an Island, the better your chances to breed special Monsters!
- Visit a Wishing Torch's Info to see how much burn time it has left! The mod also says it when you select the torch, for example "lit, 5 hours left".
- Permanently-lit Wishing Torches burn a brilliant blue! The mod says "lit permanently" instead.
- Toggle the 'Highlight' button on a Wishing Torch to show other players where to prioritize lighting! The mod reads it as "Highlight for friends: on" or "off".

## Friends, profile and community

- Sort your Friends list using filters - Favorites, Torch requests, Alphabetical order, Last Login and Level order.
- You can change your Display Name in the Profile Menu! Profile is in the island's Tab list.
- Go to the Profile Menu to choose an Avatar you've collected to represent you!
- See how many Upvotes your Island has in the Castle's Stats tab in the Info Menu.
- Show off your star status with Monikers, unlocked at Level 30!
- Join together with your friends to create a Tribe and unlock an ancient secret of the Monster World on Tribal Island!
- Record the Support ID of your account in a safe place and do not share it with others. This is helpful if we need to assist in recovering lost progress.

## Minigames and free stuff

- Think you have what it takes to play the Memory Game? Play for free daily from the Castle! Select the Castle and Tab to MEMORY. The mod says whether a free game is waiting.
- Try your luck at the Monster Scratch Ticket in the Nursery Menu, for free once a week.
- Spin Wheel prizes scale as you level up!
- Play Dipster Digs to uncover hidden Dipsters and get special Prizes!
- The 'Loony Legendary' Alcordion is the host of special Minigames!
- Earned some Starpower? Visit the StarShop in the Market to redeem it for exclusive Decorations!
- Check the StarShop for Rare Trio promotions, in which you can buy featured Rares for Starpower!
- Missed out on a Rare or Epic? Check back often - it may be available for a limited time in a special promotion!
- Still no luck getting that elusive Ethereal? Don't give up... another Breeding Bonanza promotion might be just around the corner!
- Outside of their Seasonal Events, holiday-themed Seasonal Monsters are sometimes available in Out of Season promotions!
- Spectacular Decorations and Costumes designed by Creator Experience backers are available to collect!

- Look out below! The mysterious Celestial Island and its cosmic inhabitants are yours to discover at Level 15.
- Come one, come all to the Paironormal Carnival, unlocked at Level 22!
- You can teleport Ethereal Monsters to Ethereal Island once they have reached Level 15. TELEPORT is on a selected monster's bar.
- Teleport Mythicals at Level 15 to the oneiric Mythical Island to meet the Cataliszt and breed the Dreamythicals!
- Transpose select Magicals to Magical Nexus so they can meet their maker, the mysterious Stair Shaper!
- Transpose Level 18 Paironormals from the Magical Islands to start collecting a troupe of hybrid oddities!

- Create your own Monstrous Melodies with Composer Island!
- Glowbes and notes of length on Composer Island can take your compositions to the next level!
- Let's get ready to grumble on the Colossingum, a special Island Arena!
- Every week, different Costumes are available as prizes in limited-time Quests on the Colossingum!
- Battle your way to the top Tier and beyond in the prestigious Champions' Guild in Versus Mode!
- As you complete Battle Events and boost your Colossingum Level, you'll unlock awesome tunes to enjoy in the Jukebox!

## Breeding and Elements

- Each Island has a unique family of Elements - pay close attention to a Monster's sigils to determine how to breed the Monster you want! The sigils are pictures, but the mod reads a monster's elements as words: press I with the monster selected.
- Ethereal Island has a separate family of Elements - Plasma, Mech, Shadow, Crystal, and Poison.
- Magical Sanctum has a separate family of Elements - Psychic, Faerie, Bone and Light.
- Ethereal Monsters are valuable and hard to come by. It takes a lot of luck and patience to breed one.
- The Shugabush is required for all breeding combinations on Shugabush Island.
- The bbli$zard is required for all breeding combinations on bbli$zard Island.
- Reunite the members of the Legendary Shugafam on Shugabush Island!
- Assemble the members of the Legendary bb$quad on bbli$zard Island!

## Special monster families

- Collect Keys to meet the Dipsters: Do, Re, Mi, Fa, Sol, La, and Ti.
- The 4 Dipster subspecies are Tonal, Elemental, Royal and Astral.
- Small but mighty, Dipsters can survive in a variety of environments.
- What's the word? Werdos, that's what! Get these loquacious Monsters with Relics in the Market!
- There are multiple ways to get the Relics required to collect Werdos and Fire Monsters.
- Are you ready to wake up the Wublins? Zap Eggs to Wublin Island to give these statues the spark of life!
- The Wubbox has been revealed as the creator of the Wublins!
- Wubbox are shockingly costly, it's true... but that's because they're so valuable! With great Monster, comes great Electricity.
- Rare and Epic Wubbox are unique because they perform different musical parts than their Common form!
- The Rare Wubbox has been discovered on Ethereal Island! What will its cryptic message be?
- When you place a Level 15 Epic Wubbox on Gold Island, it will assume a new powered down form!
- The Playground is a virtual world that exists inside, and is accessible from, every Wubbox.
- You can place up to 5 of each type of Celestial on Celestial Island.
- Celestials have the unique ability to ascend before their inventories are completely full.
- Wildcards can be produced during unsuccessful Early Ascension attempts on Celestial Island, or by Minor Paironormals on Paironormal Carnival.
- Light up your world with Glowbes! Conjure and Fuze to create a full spectrum of luminous possibility! With the mod, the Fuzer says what a conjure makes, and the three Glowbes are named Red, Green and Blue.
- Use the spark of life to revitalize special Vessels on Amber Island and reunite the Fire Monsters!
- Create 'Soul Links' to Monsters to stir the Titansoul to life and change a Magical Island forever!
- A Titansoul 'conducts' the landscape of its home island to add new musical parts to the song!
- Each Mythical is paired with a Dreamythical hybrid brought to life by the power of dreamcraft and inspired by its imagination!
- Use the Dish-Harmonizer to turn one Monster into two on the Ethereal Islets!
- Higher level Monsters result in more Meebs when you put them in the Dish-Harmonizer!
- Primordials are reworked 'early drafts' of Monsters that are unique to the Ethereal Islets.

## Paironormals

- Each form of a Paironormal Monster is available depending on the day and night cycle of the human world.
- Paironormal Carnival has 2 modes - Major and Minor - that each play a different version of its island song!
- If you own distinct Major and Minor forms of a Monster on Paironormal Carnival, you can Pair them together!
- A single Paironormal can only achieve full Happiness if both its Major and Minor forms are filled out on the Paironormal Carnival.
- On the Paironormal Carnival, Major Paironormals produce Starpower, while Minor Paironormals produce Wildcards!
- Upgrade the Centri-Fugue to its active state to attempt Fuguing a Paironormal to fill out its Pair!
- Each Paironormal has a unique byname used to describe its Elemental affiliation or defining attribute.
- The Legendary bbli$zard and Scallyrags the Rascal Paironormal are voiced by rap phenomenon bbno$!

## Lore

These don't change how you play, but they're fun.

- Monster-Handlers revere the teachings of Dr. Wardin E. Spurrit, a pioneer in the field of Monster-Handling.
- The fabled Book of Monsters helps you keep track of your Monster collection.
- Amber Island exists on the same higher plane as Gold Island, but it's unclear which island is older.
- Magical Sanctum exists in the same pocket dimension as Ethereal Island, although the latter is much older.
- It's believed that Magical Islands share a special connection to Outer Islands from the Dawn of Fire.
- Some Islands are imbued with the spirits of Colossals, while others are actually giant beings called Titans!
- Mythical Island exists in the dreamspace, a realm only accessible via deep dream Teleportation.
- The Ethereal Workshop is a secret laboratory hidden inside Ethereal Island and uses revolutionary Structures to harness the powers of Attunement and Synthesis.
- The Colossal Zarroë's latest ambition is a set of Ethereal Islets located in the Mirror timeline.
- The number 18 seems to hold special significance to the Stair Shaper - you'll see it reflected in many aspects of life on Magical Nexus.
- No one knows for sure how Paironormal Monsters come into being, although it's suspected that their true origins lie in the Beyond.
- The 1derland is a parallel reality where Monsters take on weird and wonderful forms.
- Despite making a home on Air Island, it's rumored that the Alcordion has also been spotted at a far-off land called... Albuquirky?
- Where do Seasonal Monsters go during the rest of the year? To the wondrous Seasonal Shanty, of course!
- Long live Fire! The hidden sanctuary of Fire Haven has been discovered, complete with long-lost Fire Monsters!
- Raise a glass... blown glass, that is! Fire Oasis is the perfect home for Fire Monsters on the surface of the Monster World!
- Psychic Island used to be called Fire Frontier, before its resident Psychic Elementals revealed themselves. Mind = blown!
- Light Island and the Aurora Monstralis seem to follow each other around the Monster World.
- If there's a place where wishes come true, Faerie Island is that place!
- Bone up on your Magical Monster knowledge on Bone Island, home of the Bone Elementals!
